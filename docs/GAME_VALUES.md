## 2. Player Progression & Chapter Level Caps

- **Hard Level Cap:** `50` (unlocked progressively across 7 Chapter Boss gates; `Attribute Gate` $= \text{PlayerLevel} \times 2$; staged `Power` ceiling $20 \to 36 \to 52 \to 66 \to 70 \to 75 \to 80$).
- **Player HP Formula:**
  $$\text{HP}(\text{Lv}) = 100 + 25 \times (\text{Lv} - 1) + \text{round}\left(15 \times 1.16^{\text{Lv} - 1}\right)$$
- **Smoothed Level-Up Cost Formula:**
  - **Levels 1–9:** $\text{round}\left(150 \times 1.75^{\text{Lv} - 1}\right)$
  - **Levels 10–25:** $\text{round}\left(18,500 \times 1.28^{\text{Lv} - 10}\right)$
  - **Levels 26–35:** $\text{round}\left(960,000 \times 1.23^{\text{Lv} - 26}\right)$

## 3. Attributes (Dynamic 3-Stage Pricing)

$$\text{Cost}(\text{lvl}) = \begin{cases}
\text{round}\left(\text{baseCost} \times 1.56^{\text{lvl}}\right) & \text{if } \text{lvl} \le S_1 \\[4pt]
\text{round}\left(\text{cost}_{S_1} \times 1.29^{\text{lvl} - S_1}\right) & \text{if } S_1 < \text{lvl} \le T \\[4pt]
\text{round}\left(\text{cost}_{T} \times \left(\frac{\text{maxCost}}{\text{cost}_{T}}\right)^{\frac{\text{lvl} - T}{N - 1 - T}}\right) & \text{if } T < \text{lvl} < N
\end{cases}$$

## 7. Rebalanced Game Modes

### Trio Gauntlet (Any Cleared Group · 30m Group Cooldown)
- 3 toughest enemies of the group (slots 3–5) back-to-back; HP carries over with a **$+20\%$ breather heal** between rounds.
- **Payout:** Kill bounties pay `0` during the run, slap coins pay **$75\%$**, and winning pays a completion bonus of **$3.25\times$ the trio's combined bounties** $\times$ `coinMult` + **3 Gems (first clear) / 1 Gem (repeats)**.
- **Scaling:** Round 2 enemies $+15\%$ HP / $+10\%$ ATK; Round 3 $+30\%$ HP / $+20\%$ ATK; **Cornerman Draft** between rounds.

### Boss Rush (Unlock: Defeat 1 Boss `#10` · 45m Cooldown)
- Fight all defeated bosses (`#10`–`#50`) in sequence; each kill pays $1.0\times$ bounty $\times$ `coinMult`.
- **Completion Bonus:** **$3.5\times$ the queue's combined bounties** $\times$ `coinMult` + **5 Gems (first clear) / 2 Gems (repeats)** + **$+1$ Soul Ember per boss** post-Dragon Lord.

### Endless Mode (Unlock: Defeat Any Enemy)
- Waves scale: enemy HP $\times 1.32$, ATK $\times 1.16$ per wave; boss every 5th wave; **$+30\%$ heal** + **Cornerman Draft** between waves.
- **Wave Payout:** `frontierBounty * (0.35 + 0.15 * wave) * coinMult`; every 5th-wave boss pays **$+1$ Gem** ($+1$ Soul Ember post-Dragon Lord).
