Privacy Policy for These Hands

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how These Hands ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

These Hands is designed as a standalone, offline-capable game. We do not require account registration, login credentials, or collect personal identifying information (such as real names, email addresses, or phone numbers) on our own backend servers.

A. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game integrates Google AdMob to display optional rewarded video advertisements (e.g., 10-minute 2X coin or damage boosts). Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device and usage data, including:

  • IP address (used to infer approximate location)
  • Mobile advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID)
  • Ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks, and video completions)
  • Device model, operating system version, and system language

Google's privacy practices are governed by the Google Advertising Privacy Policy.

B. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing / RevenueCat)

All real-money purchases for optional in-game items (such as gem packs, bundles, or VIP passes) are processed directly and securely through Google Play. We never receive, store, or have access to your payment card or banking details. Google Play and RevenueCat process purchase tokens and transaction receipts solely to credit your items.

C. Local Game Save Storage

Your game progress, unlocked skins, currency balances, attribute levels, and achievements are saved locally on your device storage. This data is not transmitted to our servers and remains on your device.

2. How We Use Information

We use the data collected by third-party SDKs strictly to:

  • Deliver gameplay functionality, rewards, and progress synchronization.
  • Serve rewarded advertisements when requested by the player.
  • Validate and fulfill in-app purchases.
  • Prevent fraud and ensure technical stability.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

  • Google Play Services & Billing: Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Google AdMob Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: RevenueCat Privacy Policy

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

These Hands does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information to third-party services through the game, please contact us so we can investigate and remove such data.

5. Data Deletion and Opt-Out Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete local game data: Go to your device Settings > Apps > These Hands > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Reset or opt out of personalized ads: Go to your device Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or enable Delete advertising ID.
