# Privacy Policy for These Hands

**Last updated: October 30, 2026**

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game **These Hands** ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how data is handled.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

"These Hands" is primarily an offline-capable client-side game. We do not require account registration or collect personal identifying information (such as your real name, email address, physical address, or phone number) directly on our own servers.

However, the game integrates trusted third-party service providers that may collect certain information automatically:

#### A. Advertising (Google AdMob)
We use Google AdMob to display rewarded video advertisements. AdMob may collect and process:
- IP address (approximate geographic location)
- Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID)
- Interaction with advertisements (ad impressions, clicks, video completions)
- Device model, operating system version, and system language

Google's privacy practices regarding advertising are governed by the [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

#### B. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing / RevenueCat)
When you purchase optional in-game items (such as gems, bundles, or VIP passes), payments are processed directly by Google Play. We do not have access to or store your credit card, debit card, or banking details.
- Transaction tokens, order IDs, and entitlement states are processed to deliver your purchased virtual items.

#### C. Local Game Save Data
Your game progression, unlocked hands, coin/gem balances, attribute levels, and settings are stored locally on your device using Android app storage. This data remains on your device unless you clear application storage or uninstall the application.

---

### 2. How Information is Used

We use collected information solely to:
- Deliver in-game gameplay, progression, and rewards.
- Provide rewarded advertisements (e.g., optional 10-minute 2x coin/damage boosts).
- Process and validate in-app purchases.
- Maintain app security, prevent fraud, and ensure game stability.

### 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

"These Hands" does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because game data is stored locally on your device, you can delete your data at any time by clearing the application storage via:
**Settings > Apps > These Hands > Storage > Clear Data**.

To reset your advertising identifier, you can do so in your Android device settings under:
**Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID**.
